## What Is SassyMartie?

SassyMartie is a discovery hub for indie SaaS tools built by real people with real problems and real rent to pay.

It is a **thrift store for software** — not a boutique, not a mall, not a VC showcase. You come in knowing it's a mixed bag. You leave having found exactly what Bob's dropshipping store needed for $7/month. Sometimes you find a Rolex between the broken toasters. That's the whole point.

We are not a marketplace. We are not a review site. We are not Product Hunt. We are not G2. We are not Flippa.

We are a **hub**. A directory. A home for the slop and the diamonds alike.

---

## Vision

> A world where a solo dev's portfolio of scrappy tools can feed them — without a pitch deck, without a LinkedIn grind, without begging for upvotes.

## Mission

> To be the permanent discovery layer for indie SaaS — where builders list their work, buyers find their tools, and word of mouth does the rest.

---

## Philosophy

### 1. The Thrift Store Doctrine
We are Goodwill for software. Stuff here ranges from barely-functional AI output to quietly genius tools built by devs who hate marketing. Both belong here. No gatekeeping. The market sorts itself. The diamonds resurface through word of mouth — always.

### 2. The Shovel, Not The Gold
We don't build the SaaS. We don't host it. We don't support it. We don't sell it. We are the **surface** where it gets discovered. That's it. Our cost is pennies. Our value is distribution. Simple.

### 3. Pennies-Per-Page, Stacked Revenue
This business runs on a CMS and a dream. Every listing is a page. Every page is indexed by Google. Every indexed page is SEO real estate that costs us nothing extra. We monetize through listing fees, promoted slots, ads, verified badges — not by taking a cut of transactions we never touch.

### 4. Zero Pretension
We don't claim these tools are enterprise-grade. We don't promise uptime SLAs. We don't do demo calls. We are honest about what we are: a curated-ish dumping ground where useful software finds a home. Users come in with eyes open. Builders list with no illusions. That's the contract.

### 5. The Portfolio Problem Is Real
A solo dev building 7 tools across 7 domains has no cross-promotion layer. Their one viral hit sends zero traffic to their other tools. SassyMartie is the **profile page that connects the dots** — like a Bandcamp artist page, but for SaaS. When one tool pops, the whole portfolio gets discovered.

### 6. Word of Mouth Is The Algorithm
We do not curate. We do not feature-gate based on "traction." We do not have an editorial committee. Good tools get shared. Bad tools get buried. The market is the algorithm. We just keep the lights on.

## Our Take on the Post-AI Era

We are living in the **Cambrian Explosion of software.**

AI coding tools have collapsed the barrier to building. Anyone with a laptop, a Claude subscription, and a real problem can ship a SaaS in a weekend. This is not a threat. This is the whole point.

Yes, most of it is slop. Yes, a lot of it barely works. Yes, some of it will be abandoned in 3 months.

**We don't care.**

Because inside that ocean of slop are tools that solve Bob's exact inventory problem for $9/month. Tools that Alice's haircut shop actually needed. Tools built by a dev in Jakarta at 2am who just wanted to scratch their own itch and figured others might have the same itch.

The post-AI era doesn't need another gatekeeper deciding what's "good enough" to deserve distribution.

It needs a **thrift store.**

That's us.

## Our Take on Enterprise

We have nothing against enterprise software.

We just don't serve it. And it doesn't serve our people.

Our people are:
- Solo devs shipping on weekends
- Small business owners with actual budgets (not procurement committees)
- Indie hackers with 5 half-alive projects
- The Bobs and Alices of the world who need tools that work, not tools that come with a dedicated Customer Success Manager

Enterprise software is built for the Fortune 500. Demo calls. Procurement cycles. "Contact us for pricing." Security reviews. IT approvals.

**Our builders don't have time for that. Our buyers don't have budget for that.**

SassyMartie is for the rest of us. The broke builders trying to make money. The small operators trying to save it. No demo calls. No pitch decks. No "we'll get back to you with a quote."

Just: here's the tool, here's the price, here's the link. Take it or leave it.

## The Do's

✅ List anything — functional AI slop to polished indie gems, all welcome
✅ Be honest about what your tool does and doesn't do
✅ Link out to your own domain — we're the landing page before the landing page
✅ Build your profile as a portfolio — list all your tools, not just the flagship
✅ Price for real people — if Bob can't afford it, it's not for Bob
✅ Let word of mouth do the work — build something useful, the rest follows
✅ Be sassy. We're called SassyMartie for a reason.

---

## The Don'ts

❌ Don't pretend your weekend project is enterprise software
❌ Don't list vaporware — it needs to exist and work (enough)
❌ Don't expect us to host, support, or maintain your tool — that's on you
❌ Don't game the listing for SEO spam — we're a hub, not a content farm
❌ Don't bring LinkedIn energy — "humbled and excited to announce" belongs elsewhere
❌ Don't expect demo calls from buyers — they came here to skip that
❌ Don't gatekeep other builders — your slop and their slop can coexist
