03 — Brand Foundation

Logo Rules

The logo is the wordmark. Never distort, recolour, or separate "Sassy" from "Martie".

✅ Do

Correct
Use on cream or white backgrounds. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "M".
Scale proportionally
Digital min: 120px wide. Print min: 1.5 inches.

❌ Never

No distortion or rotation
Never skew, stretch, or add shadows, bevels, or glows.
No unapproved colours
Never separate "Sassy" from "Martie". Never recolour to off-brand tones.

12 — Voice & Tone

Voice & Tone

Talk like the friendly senior who remembers what it felt like to be new — warm, direct, no fluff, occasionally funny but never trying hard.

"Would Martie say this to a new friend at a hackathon?" — The Martie Test
✅ Do
"Hey, glad you're here."
❌ Never
"Welcome to our AMAZING community of INCREDIBLE creators!!!"
✅ Do
"List your tool. Tell people what it does. Done."
❌ Never
"Our platform facilitates the seamless submission and discoverability of your software product."
✅ Do
"We're small but growing. And we're building this with you."
❌ Never
"The #1 AI SaaS directory trusted by thousands of world-class founders!"
✅ Do
"Too many ideas, not enough time. We know."
❌ Never
"Leverage our robust ecosystem to monetize your disruptive solution."

13 — Voice & Tone

Word Bank

The vocabulary that makes SassyMartie sound like itself, not like everyone else.

Use Freely

ship build tool scrappy real honest indie solo founder chaos get seen discover heart weird wild built different lemme in

Use Sparingly

hacker indie hacker caffeine too many ideas code

Never Use

leverage utilize seamless frictionless robust world-class synergy ecosystem monetize users stakeholders disruptive industry-leading
One rule to remember
"Users" → "people" or "builders". "Monetize" → "make money" or "get paid". Treat builders as humans, not metrics.
